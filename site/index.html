      How it works

      A hunting party, not a brute-force search.

      Checking every subset of 40 columns would take a trillion model fits. The hawks get there in about a thousand.

      01

      Every hawk is a guess

      Each hawk carries one candidate subset of your columns: a row of on/off switches, one per feature.

      02

      Guesses are scored

      A k-nearest-neighbour classifier is trained on just those columns and tested on data it hasn't seen. Fewer columns earn a small bonus.

      03

      The flock closes in

      The best subset is the "rabbit". Early on hawks scout widely; as the rabbit's energy drains they besiege it and dive, refining the best answer.

      04

      A fair final test

      The winning subset is checked on a held-out 20% of your rows and compared with using every column and with a simple statistical filter.
